About Us

Didascere Quest is an exclusive 2D pixel-art educational game created specifically for the Grade 5 students of La Naval Academy. Designed as a dedicated practice ground for their mathematics class, the game gives students an enjoyable space to apply what they have learned in school through play rather than repetitive drills. Instead of traditional worksheets, learners explore an interactive virtual village where math lessons turn into engaging minigames and quests. As they complete tasks, students earn Star Points to restore village buildings and unlock milestones, making math practice an exciting adventure they actually look forward to.
